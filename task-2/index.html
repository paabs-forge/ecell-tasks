About E-Cell

An ecosystem for turning ideas into action.

E-Cell UIET KUK works to build an entrepreneurial ecosystem where students, mentors, professionals and entrepreneurs can interact around ideas and innovation.

The focus is not only on having an idea. It is on exploring the problem, learning how to develop the idea, getting useful feedback and finding the support needed to move it forward.
